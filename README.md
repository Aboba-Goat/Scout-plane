# How To Build:
Step 1 — Cut Out All the Foam Parts

Cut each piece carefully from your polystyrene board using a hobby knife. Follow the dimensions below exactly.

Nose (front of fuselage)
 Cut a strip that tapers from 32 cm on one end down to 7.5 cm on the other, and is 8 cm wide. Fold it into the nose shape.
 
Middle fuselage
 Cut a strip 34 cm long × 9 cm wide. Fold into shape.
 
Rear fuselage
 Cut a strip 34 cm long, tapering from 21.5 cm wide on one end to 11 cm wide on the other. Fold into shape.
Wings (make 2 identical pieces)

 Cut a strip 24 cm long × 16 cm wide. Shape it into an airfoil profile (curved top, flat bottom) matching the 3D model.
Then, measured 2.5 cm from one end, cut out a slot 10 cm long × 2 cm wide — this is your aileron. Fold the aileron so it's 0.4 cm tall in the middle when bent. Repeat for the second wing.

Elevator
 Cut a strip 11.5 cm long × 9 cm wide and shape into an airfoil. Then cut one side at a 5° angle so that edge measures 11 cm total. The angled side will be glued to the fuselage later.
 
Horizontal stabilizer
 Cut a strip 27 cm long × 3 cm wide and fold into its final shape.
 
Vertical stabilizer + rudder
 Cut a strip 17 cm long, tapering from 10 cm wide on one end to 8 cm wide on the other. Shape it into a cone.
On one side of the cone, make a 15° indent into the foam. Make sure there's 3.5 cm clearance between the bottom and top of the cone. Then, 1 cm from the top, cut out a slot 6 cm long × 2 cm wide — this is your rudder.

Retention pin tubes
 Cut hollow tube sticks to these lengths:
3 cm × 1
4 cm × 1
6 cm × 1
23 cm × 1
30 cm × 2
The 23 cm tube gets permanently glued to the back of the fuselage. The two 30 cm tubes should overlap by 1 cm in the middle when the plane is assembled — this is intentional so the retention pin locks both sections together.

Refer to the 3D model for exact tube placement positions.

Step 2 — Install the Electronics
Motor — Mount the brushless motor in the nose of the fuselage as shown in the 3D model. Connect it to the ESC.
Power chain — Connect the ESC to the BEC 5V module, then connect the BEC to the receiver.
Battery — Glue the battery in place and connect it to the receiver.
Camera — Mount the FPV camera and connect it to the receiver.
Servos — Install one servo per control surface (ailerons, elevator, rudder) in the positions shown in the 3D model. Connect each servo to the receiver.

Step 3 — Attach Control Surfaces
Hot glue the ailerons onto the wings and connect them to their servo horns with hinges.
Do the same for the elevator.
Hot glue the vertical stabilizer to the rear of the fuselage.
Important: Do NOT glue the fuselage sections together — except for the front (nose) and middle section. The remaining sections are meant to be held together with the retention pins so the plane can be disassembled.

Step 4 — Final Assembly
Slide all the fuselage sections together using the retention pins. Lock everything in place and you're done — your plane is ready to fly!
